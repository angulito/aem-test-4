Casual Cool

The Polo Gets a Glow Up

Tennis polos aren’t just for the court anymore — they’re a whole lifestyle. The Ace Pro Court Polo takes that classic silhouette and gives it fresh energy: performance fabrics, bold colorways, and a fit that moves from baseline rallies to brunch without missing a beat.

This isn’t your dad’s boxy polo. It’s built for the way you actually live — active, social, and always on the move.

Why This Polo Hits Different

A great polo does more than look good. It works hard so you don’t have to think about it. Here’s what makes the Ace Pro stand out:

Fabric that breathes — Recycled polyester and elastane in a four-way stretch microfiber. It wicks moisture, dries fast, and keeps you cool during long rallies or long walks through the city.

A collar that stays put — Heat-bonded construction means no curling, no collapsing, no sad floppy collar after the first wash. It looks as sharp at set point as it does at happy hour.

UPF 50+ sun protection — Built right into the fabric. You’re blocking 98% of UV rays without layering on extra sunscreen. Perfect for outdoor sessions that stretch into golden hour.

Flatlock seams + odor resistance — Zero chafe, zero funk. Silver-ion antimicrobial treatment holds up for 50+ washes. Your post-match confidence? Effortless.

Alex’s Court-to-Street Edit

Alex Rivera lives in polos from spring through fall. His style rule is simple: if a shirt can’t handle both a tennis match and a rooftop hangout, it doesn’t make the rotation.

“I’m all about pieces that do double duty. The Ace Pro is the real deal — I’ll play a full set, throw on some chinos, and head straight to dinner. The stretch is unreal and the colors pop without trying too hard.”

The Colorway Lineup

  • Classic navy — Clean, bold, goes with literally everything
  • Coral pop — A statement that turns heads at the park or the party
  • Teal energy — Fresh and vibrant, perfect for standing out on court
  • Crisp white — Timeless tennis vibes, reimagined with modern stretch

From Baseline to Brunch

The best part about the Ace Pro? One shirt, three completely different looks.

Match mode — Pair it with performance shorts and your favorite court shoes. You’re locked in and looking sharp.

City mode — Swap in chinos and clean sneakers. Roll up to lunch and own the whole room.

Night mode — Layer a lightweight jacket over the polo, add some accessories, and suddenly you’re the most interesting person at the rooftop.

“The real flex is showing up to the restaurant in the same shirt you just played tennis in — and nobody can tell. That’s confidence.” — Jordan Ellis, Trend Spotter

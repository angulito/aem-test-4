Game on, style up

By Taylor Brooks

June 5, 2024 • 4 min read

Sport Mode

Athleisure Isn’t Going Anywhere

If there’s one trend that’s proven its staying power, it’s athleisure. But this isn’t your basic gym-to-brunch look. Today’s sport-inspired fashion is elevated, intentional, and surprisingly versatile.

The Sport Mode Mindset

Casey Drew from Fresh Fit puts it best: “Sporty, casual, or glam—there’s always a look for my mood.” That flexibility is exactly what makes athleisure so appealing to young fashion lovers.

The Updated Athleisure Kit

  • Track pants with bold side stripes or color blocking
  • Cropped hoodies in oversized fits
  • Retro trainers — the chunkier, the better
  • Sports bras as tops layered under oversized shirts
  • Windbreakers in bold, graphic prints

Beyond the Gym

The key to great athleisure is treating it like any other fashion category. Think about proportions, color coordination, and accessorizing. A matching track set with clean sneakers and gold jewelry? That’s a look. Baggy sweats with a fitted top and a structured bag? Also a look.

The Brand Factor

Young trendsetters are mixing heritage sportswear brands with independent labels, creating outfits that feel both familiar and fresh. It’s not about logos—it’s about finding pieces that move well and look even better.

Active Style, Real Life

Whether you’re actually working out, running errands, or meeting friends, sport-inspired style keeps you comfortable and confident. The best outfits are the ones you can live your life in—and athleisure delivers on that promise every single time.

“Fashion should move with you, not against you. That’s why sport-inspired style will always win.” — Riley Quinn, Sunset Crew

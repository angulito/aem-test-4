Slide into summer — flip flops that serve

By Taylor Kim

February 22, 2026 • 4 min read

Beach Vibes

The Slide Is Everything

Let’s get one thing straight — flip flops aren’t lazy. The right pair takes you from boardwalk to brunch, from sandy toes to sunset drinks.

And this summer, the slide game is stronger than ever. Think elevated: platform soles, woven straps, bold colors that pop against sun-kissed skin. Gone are the days of sad rubber flip flops from the gas station — today’s slides are a whole mood.

Taylor’s Beach-to-Street Edit

Taylor Kim lives in flip flops from May through September. Her rule is simple: if a pair cannot handle both sand and sidewalk, it does not make the cut.

“I used to think flip flops were just for the beach. Now they’re literally my go-to for everything — coffee runs, beach days, rooftop hangouts. The trick is finding a pair that looks intentional, not accidental.”

The Must-Have Rotation

  • Platform slides in neutral tones — instant elevation (literally)
  • Woven leather sandals that look better with every wear
  • Bold-color rubber slides for beach days and pool parties
  • Canvas strap flip flops for that effortless campus energy

Own the Beach Day Look

Beach style has nothing to do with trying hard. The whole point is looking like you didn’t try at all — even when you absolutely did.

Pair your flip flops with linen shorts and an oversized button-down, roll the sleeves, and skip the socks. Throw on a bucket hat and some chunky sunglasses for a look that works from noon to night.

The best beach looks have one thing in common: they move with you. No stiff fabrics, no fussy details — just easy pieces that let you focus on good vibes, good company, and good weather.

Color That Pops

This summer’s flip flop palette goes hard. The trends are landing in three lanes:

Earth tones — sandy beige, terracotta, warm olive. These work with literally everything and only look better with a tan.

Ocean brights — coral, turquoise, seafoam. Bold choices that make a statement without trying too hard.

Classic black — because sometimes you need that one pair that goes with everything, no explanation needed.

“The best outfit is the one you don’t have to think about. Flip flops, good shorts, and confidence — that’s the whole formula.” — Jordan Ellis, Trend Spotter

From Sand to Street

The real flex is one pair of flip flops styled three completely different ways.

Beach mode — slides with board shorts and a tank top. Zero overthinking required.

Brunch mode — same slides with linen pants and a fresh tee. Roll up to the restaurant and own the whole room.

Sunset mode — add a lightweight overshirt and some accessories, and suddenly you become the person everyone wants to hang with.

The Comfort Factor

Real talk — comfort is not just nice to have, it is everything. Nobody is serving looks while limping around in flip flops that dig into their feet. Look for cushioned footbeds, arch support, and straps that sit right. Your feet will thank you after a full day of beach hopping and city strolling.

Casey Drew swears by it: “I’m all about the gym-to-street transition, but summer? It’s flip-flop-to-everywhere. If my slides can handle a beach volleyball game and still look fresh at dinner, that’s a win.”

Your Move

Summer is calling and your feet deserve better than those worn-out sneakers. Grab a pair that makes you feel something, rock them everywhere, and own the look.
